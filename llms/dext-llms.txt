/clea# Dext

> Dext is bookkeeping automation software for businesses, accountants and bookkeepers. It captures receipts, invoices and bank statements, extracts and validates the data, and publishes it into accounting software such as Xero, QuickBooks and Sage.

Dext serves over 700,000 customers worldwide across the UK, US, Canada, Australia, France, South Africa and beyond. The site is available in multiple regions; the canonical English (global) pages are listed below under https://dext.com/en/, followed by region-specific pages for individual markets (e.g. UK, France). Pricing and promotions vary by region and change frequently — always read the linked pricing pages for current figures rather than relying on cached values.
